Meet The Founder

Feku Eritei Emmanuel is a visionary leader, communication expert, author, and youth mentor who focuses on raising purpose-driven young leaders. As Team Lead of Eden Mentorship Academy, he runs Eden mentorship platform dedicated to leadership development, identity discovery, and personal growth for teenagers, helping them live with clarity, vision, and impact.

He has extensive experience in public speaking coaching, leadership training, podcast hosting, and humanitarian outreach, through which he has impacted hundreds of young people via programs, workshops, and digital content. He is also the author of The Visionary Teenager, and actively participates in ministry, evangelism, and community development initiatives across Nigeria, reinforcing his commitment to youth empowerment and transformational leadership.
