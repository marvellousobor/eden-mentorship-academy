Why Counselling Matters

We understand that life can be tough. During such moments, you need a shoulder you can lean on, and a safe haven where you can find solace and motivation to get going.

Whether you're going through addiction, abuse, trauma, identity confusion, or indecisiveness, the Eden Academy Counselling Unit is here for you.

Do not go through life alone when there's a haven you can find safety and direction.

Counselling is FREE

We believe everyone deserves access to quality counselling support without financial barriers.

Areas We Help With

Our compassionate counsellors are trained to help you navigate through various life challenges

Addiction Support

Struggling with substance abuse or behavioral addictions? We provide compassionate support to help you break free and reclaim your life.

Abuse & Trauma

If you've experienced physical, emotional, or sexual abuse, we're here to help you heal and rebuild your confidence.

Identity Confusion

Struggling with who you are or where you fit in? Our counsellors help you discover and embrace your true identity.

Indecisiveness

Can't make important life decisions? We help you clarify your thoughts, values, and goals to move forward confidently.

Relationship Issues

Having difficulty with family, friends, or romantic relationships? We help you build healthier connections.

Mental Health & Wellness

Dealing with anxiety, depression, or stress? Our counsellors provide strategies for mental wellness and emotional balance.
